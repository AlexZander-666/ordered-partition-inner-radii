# Scope of the author revision

The starting source is the public version 1.0.0 by Bowen Zuo. Its PDF SHA-256 is b83052e5cd5392477cbfc140580f49d88a3be5b84642feb3b1dae0a98a73f4e0 and TeX archive SHA-256 is 872a9b634ca26b53e61038d3f96d9dc5390e3380570934fad8edadaf53f8dda8. [Public GitHub release](https://github.com/AlexZander-666/ordered-partition-inner-radii/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Public version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24573157)

The new version extends the nearest-distance and sinc results to j=2,3, pairs these two thresholds with their exact finite-rank barrier, and keeps the explicit logarithmic bound for j≥4. The added proof uses the threshold-two half-plane inequality, the threshold-three signed quadratic integral kernel, and a monotone first-block recurrence. The known complete root lists at thresholds two and three are cited where they connect the real zero-free interval to all complex zeros. The separate work on a positive fraction of ordered roots concerns different quantifiers. [Published threshold-two proof](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24571210) · [Published threshold-three proof](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24571945)

The original object is the ordered sequence of labelled blocks. The SymCat entry concerns precisely the number of blocks of size at least j among ordered partitions. Fang's preprint concerns unordered partitions and is cited for context. [Alexandersson's catalog](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#set-partitions-and-trees) · [Fang's preprint](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.01129v1)
